OUR FIRST PRODUCT

Meal planning,
without the daily guesswork.

Meal Planner · In development

Deciding what to cook every day can take more time and energy than it should. Meal planning also means juggling food preferences, ingredients, shopping lists, and the weekly budget.

We're developing Meal Planner to help busy individuals and households organize their weekly dinners around their tastes and needs, with a clearer idea of what to buy and how much it may cost.

✳Planned features

Less planning. More living.

A simpler way to organize dinners for the week.

  • ✓Personalized weekly dinner plans
  • ✓Recipes and ingredient information
  • ✓One consolidated grocery shopping list
  • ✓Estimated grocery costs
  • ✓Flexibility to adjust meals and plans

Meal Planner has not yet launched. These are planned features and may change during development.

ABOUT US

Independent by design.

Go Mobile is an independent software product studio operated by Go Mobile Inc., a corporation registered in Delaware, United States.

We take products from an initial idea through design, development, publishing, and ongoing improvement. Each application may serve a different audience or category, but our objective stays the same: make software genuinely useful.

We're at the beginning of that journey. Meal Planner is our first product in development, and we plan to explore other practical app ideas over time.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Technology should make life easier.

Useful software doesn't have to be complicated. Sometimes an application that saves a few minutes a day or removes one repetitive decision can make a meaningful difference. We want to build products that are practical, approachable, and designed around real human needs.
